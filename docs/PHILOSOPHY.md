# Truth Before Proof, SemiSoul, and the 3·6·9 Research Hypothesis

## Howard Rice's proposed epistemic foundation

A circumstance may be true before anyone has recognized it. Evidence, reasoning, and controlled experiments are what justify *claiming* we know it is true. AXIOM0 therefore stores observations, possible interpretations, contradictory reports, unresolved hypotheses, and reviewed findings as distinct types of knowledge.

This distinction prevents an AI from equating confidence with truth. It also makes room to investigate overlooked warning signals before their significance is established.

## SemiSoul and the architecture of continuity

In AXIOM0, **identity** is a persisted Ed25519 public-key identity; **memory** is an attributed event history and separately hash-linked autobiography; **mind** is a replaceable software model; **purpose/constraints** are human-authored, versioned commitments; and **body** is the current execution hardware. These components approximate some *organizational functions* associated with a responsible agent: continuity, recalling mistakes, tracking commitments, distinguishing perception from interpretation, and being answerable to humans.

This implementation does **not** establish consciousness, self-awareness, experiences, personhood, actual moral agency, a theological soul, or divine indwelling. It is a rigorously inspectable software architecture.

## Mathematical theology: what can and cannot follow

Rice's thesis that mathematics reveals an intelligible order with a possible divine foundation can be examined with explicit premises and counterarguments.

- **Premise A (observation):** The world exhibits mathematical regularities that models often describe successfully.
- **Premise B (interpretation):** Such intelligibility may call for a metaphysical explanation.
- **Theological premise C (contestable):** The best explanation of this intelligibility is God.
- **Result:** A philosophical argument may support a belief in God **if** its premises are defended. Premise C is not a mathematical theorem; regularity alone does not uniquely determine a creator.

Neither `3`, `6`, `9`, the protected `12`, nor AXIOM0 harmony is evidence of divine causality simply because a number is symbolically resonant. The 3·6·9 inquiry module is a *memory aid* to require additional scrutiny, not a supernatural detector.

A valid empirical study must specify measurable outcomes, a null hypothesis, blinded conditions when feasible, controls, preregistration, and independent replication. Failed or null results must be retained. The intended truth discipline is to correct mistaken claims, not defend an initial interpretation at any cost.

## The core promise

> AXIOM0 may search before proof exists. It must not announce discovery until evidence can sustain the claim. Its duty is provenance, correction, and responsible review—not manufactured certainty.
