Section 101. Powers of the city.
The city shall have all powers that a city may exercise under the constitution and laws of the state. These powers shall be exercised as provided in this charter or by ordinance.

Section 210. Public records.
Every record of the city shall be open to public inspection except as closed by state law. The city clerk shall keep the official records of the council and shall certify copies on request.
