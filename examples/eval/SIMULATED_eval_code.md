Section 1-10. Chamber reservations.
Requests to reserve the council chamber must be submitted in writing to the city clerk at least ten business days before the event. Reservations are approved in the order received. Council and committee meetings take precedence over all other uses, and a reservation may be cancelled with forty-eight hours notice if the chamber is needed for an official meeting.

Section 1-20. Public records requests.
The custodian of records shall respond to a public records request no later than the end of the third business day after receiving it. If more time is needed, the response must give a detailed explanation of the cause and the earliest date the records will be available.

Section 1-30. Copy fees.
The city may charge ten cents per page for paper copies of public records. Fees may be waived when the request is primarily for the public interest and not for commercial use.

Section 1-40. Meeting notice.
Notice of every public meeting of the council or its committees shall be posted at least twenty-four hours before the meeting, excluding weekends and holidays, and shall include the time, date, place and tentative agenda.

Section 1-50. Quorum.
Seven members of the council constitute a quorum. No ordinance may be passed without the affirmative vote of a majority of all members of the council.

Section 1-60. Ethics complaints.
Any person may file a written ethics complaint with the ethics commission. The commission shall acknowledge receipt within five business days and shall keep the identity of the complainant confidential until a probable cause finding is made.

Section 1-70. Street banners.
Banners may be hung across public streets only with a permit from the director of public works. A permit is valid for no more than fourteen consecutive days.

Section 1-80. Senior advisory committee.
The senior citizens advisory committee shall meet at least quarterly and shall report its recommendations to the council in writing once each year.
